AquaMed — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

AquaMed ("the app") helps you keep track of your water intake and medication times. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it is kept.

In short

  • No account. You don't need to sign up or log in.
  • Your data stays on your phone. Nothing is sent to our servers or to third parties.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking.
  • You can delete your data at any time.

What information is handled?

Only what you enter, stored only on your device:

  • Your name (for the in-app greeting)
  • Weight and (optional) height — to suggest a daily water goal
  • Water and drink logs
  • Your medicines: name, dose, notes, times, schedule, stock, priority, (optional) photo of the package
  • Records of doses taken or skipped
  • Sleep hours, water goal, the fluid limit from your doctor (if you enter one)
  • Your emergency contact's name and phone number (if you enter one)
  • App preferences (language, theme, reminder settings)

Permissions and why

  • Notifications: to show medication and water reminders.
  • Alarms & reminders (Android): so medication reminders arrive on time.
  • Run at startup: so reminders survive a phone restart.
  • Camera / Photos (optional): only when you choose to add a photo of a medicine package. The photo is copied into the app's own folder on your device.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback and notifications.

Is information shared?

No. The app does not send any personal data over the internet. The following happen only when you choose to:

  • Tell my contact: when a dose is late and you tap the button, your phone's SMS or WhatsApp app opens with a ready message. You send it; the app never sends messages by itself.
  • Share weekly summary / export report (PDF, CSV): shared with the app you pick (e-mail, messaging, etc.).
  • Back up: all your data is written to a single file and saved wherever you choose (e.g. Google Drive, e-mail). Where you keep that file is up to you.

The privacy policies of the apps used for these actions (WhatsApp, e-mail, Drive, etc.) apply to them.

Deleting your data

  • Settings → "Delete all my data" permanently removes everything at once.
  • Uninstalling the app also deletes all of its data from your phone.
  • Individual water and dose records can be deleted in "History" inside the app.
  • Backup files you created are yours to delete.

Children

The app is not intended for people under 18.

Medical disclaimer

AquaMed is a reminder tool and does not give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. The interaction check covers only a limited set of known interactions; no warning does not mean medicines are safe together. Talk to your doctor or pharmacist about every decision regarding your medicines.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version is published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
