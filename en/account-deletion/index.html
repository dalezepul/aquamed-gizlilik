Delete your AquaMed account and data

If you created an optional AquaMed account (Google or e-mail), you can delete your account and your cloud data in either of the ways below.

1. In the app (fastest)

  • Open AquaMed.
  • Go to Settings → Account & backup.
  • Tap Delete account and cloud data and confirm (if you signed in with e-mail you will be asked for your password, with Google you will re-verify your Google account).

Deletion happens immediately.

2. If you cannot use the app (request by e-mail)

  • Send an e-mail from the address linked to your account to dalezepul@gmail.com.
  • Subject: Account deletion
  • We delete your account and cloud data within 30 days at the latest and confirm by e-mail.

What is deleted?

  • Your AquaMed account (sign-in details: e-mail, Google account ID)
  • Your cloud backup (medication, dose, water and measurement records, settings)
  • Your caregiver pairings and the shared dose plan, dose events and temporary commands tied to them

What remains?

  • Data on your phone. It exists only on your device; it is removed when you uninstall the app or use Settings → Data & privacy → "Delete all my data".
  • Ad, Google Play purchase and crash-report data are subject to Google's own processes. We keep no other records.

See the privacy policy for details.
